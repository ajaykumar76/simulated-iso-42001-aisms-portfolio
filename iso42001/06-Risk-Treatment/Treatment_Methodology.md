# Risk treatment methodology

## Scenario and purpose
Treatment choices include avoiding activity, modifying design/context, introducing safeguards, sharing risk through governed contracts, or retaining only with authorised documented approval.

## Required process and records
Example: ranking bias requires representative evaluation, monitoring where lawful, no automatic rejection and independent review. Prompt injection requires isolation, constrained permissions and adversarial testing. Traceability requires versioned model/configuration context.

## Status and limitations
Closure requires implementation evidence, effectiveness test, residual reassessment and authorised approval. No implementation is claimed.
