# Residual risk review

## Scenario and purpose
Confirm treatment implementation, evidence, operation over a defined period/sample, exceptions and revised likelihood/impact rationale.

## Required process and records
Record original ID, action evidence, test result, residual score, rationale, open conditions, approver, date and next trigger. Reopen where controls fail or context changes.
