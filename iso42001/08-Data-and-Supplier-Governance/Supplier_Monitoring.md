# Supplier monitoring plan

## Scenario and purpose
Trigger review on renewal, subprocessor or model change, hosting-region change, security incident, outage, assurance expiry or material terms change.

## Required process and records
Review notices, incidents, assurance, performance, model documentation, data handling and open actions. Reassess downstream risks; pause sharing or disable affected feature if exposure cannot be controlled.

## Status and limitations
Quarterly/annual cadences in the register are proposed placeholders, not approved schedules. No monitoring cycle is complete.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
