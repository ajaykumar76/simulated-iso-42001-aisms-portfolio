# AI data governance plan

## Scenario and purpose
Potential data includes CVs, contact details, employment/education history, communications, recruiter annotations, prompts/outputs, evaluation data and telemetry. Free text may disclose sensitive information unexpectedly.

## Required process and records
Define purpose and roles; minimise fields; record provenance and lineage; control access; set retention/deletion; separate production from test data; assess transfers/subprocessors; prohibit unassessed secondary model-training use.

## Status and limitations
Required artefacts include data inventory, flow, access matrix, retention schedule, quality results and privacy screening. No live data processing is represented.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
