# AI data quality checks

## Scenario and purpose
Assess accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, validity, representativeness, provenance and fitness for intended purpose. Compare extracted fields to source CVs; test formats/languages; identify missing, duplicate and stale records.

## Required process and records
Record dataset/version, sample, method, metric, subgroup limitations, reviewer, date and corrective action. Confidence scores alone do not prove quality. Sensitive attributes require lawful and ethical basis.

## Status and limitations
No dataset has been tested. Never upload real applicant data to this public repository.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
