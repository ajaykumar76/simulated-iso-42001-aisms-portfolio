# Supplementary impact assessment

## Scenario and purpose
This second register considers data quality, contestability, recruiter understanding, accessibility, purpose limitation, sensitive-data minimisation, meaningful review and lifecycle change. It complements the primary stakeholder-oriented register.

## Required process and records
Rows link to risk IDs and are prompts for further analysis. They do not replace privacy, fundamental-rights or other legally required assessments. Determine the organisation's role, deployment context and obligations with qualified personnel.

## Status and limitations
Simulated supplementary register; no consultation, testing or formal approval has occurred.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
