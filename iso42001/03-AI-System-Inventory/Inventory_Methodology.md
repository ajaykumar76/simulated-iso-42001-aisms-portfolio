# AI system inventory methodology

## Scenario and purpose
Include systems using machine learning, statistical inference or generative AI to produce predictions, recommendations, content or decisions. Record unique ID, owner, purpose, users, affected persons, decision influence, lifecycle, data, model/version, interfaces, oversight, monitoring and linked assessments.

## Required process and records
Create records at proposal/procurement; update at design, testing, release, model/prompt/supplier change, incident, retirement and review. Mark unknown fields as unknown with an owner; never guess.

## Status and limitations
Inventory completeness should be checked quarterly and on material change. Methodology is not approved.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
