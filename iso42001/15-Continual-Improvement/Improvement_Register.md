# Continual improvement register

## Scenario and purpose
Candidate improvements: verify system dossiers; develop requirement-level mapping; build synthetic evaluation data; test human review/appeal; improve supplier/model traceability; strengthen evidence provenance; conduct incident tabletop.

## Required process and records
Capture source, benefit, risk, owner, priority, dependencies, approval, outcome metric and effectiveness evidence. A document addition alone is not proof of improvement.

## Status and limitations
All items are candidate backlog; none is marked complete.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
