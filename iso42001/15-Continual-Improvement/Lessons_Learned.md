# Lessons learned

## Scenario and purpose
A passing CSV validator proves structure, not AIMS effectiveness. Control title is not control design. Planned evidence differs from verified evidence. Risk arithmetic is not risk rationale. Impact analysis must consider affected people and contestability. Supplier categories are not due diligence. Checklists are not workpapers. Provenance must be disclosed.

## Required process and records
Reassess lessons after a documented tabletop, synthetic evaluation or independent review. Record actual observations and do not retroactively claim activities that did not occur.
