# Risk acceptance and escalation

## Scenario and purpose
Proposed authority: low risk may be recommended by system owner; medium requires risk-owner review; high requires top management or delegated authority; critical defaults to no release or pause until exposure is reduced or an exceptional lawful decision is documented.

## Required process and records
Acceptance record needs inherent/residual rationale, controls, stakeholders, constraints, decision maker, date, expiry and conditions. Never accept an issue prohibited by law or contract. Lack of evidence is not a reason to accept.

## Status and limitations
All current risks are illustrative and open. No residual score or risk acceptance is approved.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
