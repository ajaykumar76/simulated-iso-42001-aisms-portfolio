# AI risk assessment methodology

## Scenario and purpose
Assess each system, intended purpose, lifecycle stage and affected group. Write cause → event → consequence scenarios. Current illustrative likelihood and impact scales are 1–5; score is their product. Bands: Low 1–4, Medium 5–9, High 10–15, Critical 16–25.

## Required process and records
Explain exposure, frequency, safeguards and uncertainty for likelihood; severity, scale, reversibility, affected groups and duration for impact. Record treatment, owner, due date, residual assessment and approval.

## Status and limitations
These bands are training assumptions, not a prescribed ISO scale. Formal approval and calibration are outstanding.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
