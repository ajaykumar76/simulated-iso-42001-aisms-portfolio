# Interested parties

## Scenario and purpose
Applicants need fair treatment, privacy, transparency, correction and human contact. Customers need reliable service and clear responsibilities. Recruiters need understandable outputs and authority to override. Workers need competence and escalation routes. Suppliers need clear requirements. Regulators expect applicable obligations to be met.

## Required process and records
Classify expectations as legal, contractual, customer, stakeholder or voluntary; do not assume every expectation is a legal requirement.

## Status and limitations
Engagement methods may include user research, accessible notices, customer onboarding, worker training, supplier reviews and complaint analysis. No consultation has occurred in this simulation.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
