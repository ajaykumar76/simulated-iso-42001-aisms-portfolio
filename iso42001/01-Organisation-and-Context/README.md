# Organisation and context

## Scenario and purpose
The fictional MaltaAI Solutions Ltd. provides a B2B recruitment SaaS with CV extraction, candidate ranking recommendations, candidate summaries, a recruitment chatbot and aggregate analytics.

## Required process and records
Employment decisions affect applicants' opportunities; customer configurations vary; CVs may contain sensitive free text; models and suppliers change; applicants may not understand or contest automated recommendations.
