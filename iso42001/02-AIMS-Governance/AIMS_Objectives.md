# AIMS objectives

## Scenario and purpose
Proposed objectives: complete system inventory; assess all in-scope system risks; record human review for eligible ranking workflows; assess material supplier changes before adoption; exercise incident readiness annually; and establish traceability for sampled outputs.

## Required process and records
Define numerator, denominator, data source, owner, reporting period and exclusions before measuring. Targets are not achieved results.

## Status and limitations
No baseline, measured performance or management approval exists. Owners and targets must be confirmed in a scenario review.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
