# Roles and responsibilities

## Scenario and purpose
Top management: direction, approval and resources. AIMS coordinator: scope, objectives and reporting. AI system owner: intended purpose and lifecycle. Risk owner: risk/treatment. Data owner: provenance, quality and retention. Security lead: security and incidents. Privacy/legal adviser: obligations. Supplier owner: due diligence. Human oversight lead: reviewer competence and override. Auditor: independent evaluation.

## Required process and records
Separate action ownership from approval and verification. A person should not approve their own high-impact treatment without independent review. Where small-team constraints prevent separation, document compensating checks.

## Status and limitations
Role holders are not appointed; this is a proposed role model.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
