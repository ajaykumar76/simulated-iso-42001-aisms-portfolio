# Document and record control

## Scenario and purpose
Controlled documents require ID, title, owner, version, status, approval authority, effective date, review date and change history. Draft, review, approve, publish, periodically review and archive superseded versions.

## Required process and records
Records must be attributable, legible, retrievable, protected and retained according to an approved schedule. Never place applicant data, credentials or supplier-confidential information in this public repository.

## Status and limitations
Branch protection and peer review can support repository governance, but a passing CI run is not document approval. This procedure is proposed only.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
