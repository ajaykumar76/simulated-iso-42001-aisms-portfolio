# Evidence collection plan

## Scenario and purpose
Define the control objective and evidence question; identify source/owner; collect a dated versioned artefact; record provenance, period and access restrictions; review authenticity/completeness/relevance; retain securely.

## Required process and records
Potential artefacts include approved policies, access reviews, tests, change tickets, supplier assurance, training, exercises and model evaluations. Do not commit personal data, secrets or confidential supplier material.

## Status and limitations
All tracker entries are planned placeholders. No evidence is supplied.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
