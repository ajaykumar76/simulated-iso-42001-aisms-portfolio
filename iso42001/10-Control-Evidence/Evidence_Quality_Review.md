# Evidence quality review

## Scenario and purpose
Assess authenticity, integrity, relevance, population/period coverage, timeliness, reproducibility and confidentiality.

## Required process and records
Use status vocabulary consistently: Planned (not collected), Received (not reviewed), Reviewed (examined with limitations), Verified (sufficient for a specific test), Rejected (unsuitable).
