# Internal audit methodology

## Scenario and purpose
Define criteria, scope, period, auditee, independence, risk and sampling. Test through interview, observation, document/configuration review and reperformance where appropriate.

## Required process and records
Record population, sample rationale, steps, evidence, exceptions and reviewer. Findings separate condition, criteria, evidence, cause and effect. Do not infer root cause or conclusion from checklist response alone.

## Status and limitations
Verify correction and effectiveness before closure. No testing has been performed.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
