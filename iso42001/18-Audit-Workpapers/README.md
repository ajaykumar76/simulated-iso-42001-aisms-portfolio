# Desk-based audit workpapers — training simulation

**These are constructed desk exercises, not records of a real internal audit.** No interviews, live-system inspection, supplier confirmation, production sampling or operating-effectiveness tests occurred. Checklist remains Not performed / Not tested; findings remain open and are not independently validated.

## WP-01 — risk scoring reperformance
Objective: check arithmetic and local band consistency. Criteria: portfolio's illustrative 1–5 likelihood × 1–5 impact method; bands are local assumptions, not ISO-prescribed. Sample AIR-002. Recalculate 4 × 5 = 20 and compare band; inspect residual acceptance. Synthetic result: arithmetic 20; provisional band Critical; residual risk not approved. Limitation: no calibration, approved method or owner interview. Follow-up: approve anchors and obtain authorised decisions.

## WP-02 — impact assessment trace
Trace AIR-002 to AII-002/AII-007; inspect affected parties, harm, safeguards and triggers. Synthetic result: applicants/recruiters and proposed evaluation/review/escalation recorded; consultation and residual impact undetermined. No affected-person consultation, performance data or legal analysis. Follow-up: competent assessment and stakeholder engagement.

## WP-03 — supplier due diligence
Sample SUP-002 foundation-model provider category. Inspect data flow, assurance, contract, subprocessors, change and exit terms. Synthetic result: possible prompt/applicant-data transfer identified; retention, training use, location, subprocessors and assurance unknown; status Not assessed. No actual supplier or contract. Follow-up: identify supplier, obtain authoritative evidence and document decision.

## WP-04 — human oversight design walkthrough
Sample EVD-SYN-008. Trace output to source review, criteria review, rationale, override, escalation and applicant challenge. Synthetic result: proposed gates lack competence thresholds, mandatory records, override analytics, response targets and technical enforcement. No interface walkthrough or production logs. Follow-up: implement and test using synthetic accounts.

## WP-05 — traceability reconstruction
Sample DEMO-104 in EVD-SYN-009. Link request, system, model/config, output and override. Synthetic result: identifiers exist in invented sequence but dataset/version hash, criteria snapshot, authenticated actor, integrity proof and retention/access metadata are missing. No logs inspected. Follow-up: define schema and perform authorised reconstruction test.

## WP-06 — incident tabletop design review
Sample EVD-SYN-010. Map injects to detection, severity, owner, containment, preservation, impact, notification, recovery and lessons. Synthetic result: response stages described; responders, contact tree, time objectives, decision matrix, recovery criteria and results absent. No exercise conducted. Follow-up: approve playbook, run tabletop and track corrective actions.

A real workpaper needs objective, criteria, population, sample rationale, procedure, source reference, observed facts, result, exception, limitation, preparer/reviewer and follow-up. Never convert a planned or synthetic test into a completed audit result.
