# AI incident response procedure

## Scenario and purpose
Triggers include harmful/discriminatory output, ranking/extraction error at scale, prompt injection, data disclosure, drift, traceability failure, unauthorised model change, supplier compromise or oversight failure.

## Required process and records
Detect/report; triage; contain/pause; preserve privacy-aware evidence; assess impact and notification obligations with qualified advisers; communicate; restore only after review; perform root-cause analysis and track actions.

## Status and limitations
No incident or exercise is claimed.
