# Nonconformity management procedure

## Scenario and purpose
Record criterion, objective evidence, condition, affected process/system, date and reporter. Distinguish nonconformity from improvement opportunity or unverified concern.

## Required process and records
Contain and correct; address consequences; determine whether similar issues exist; assess incident escalation. Assign owner and due date. Independent reviewer verifies effectiveness.

## Status and limitations
Do not label a simulated observation as a real nonconformity. Current register entries are illustrative.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
