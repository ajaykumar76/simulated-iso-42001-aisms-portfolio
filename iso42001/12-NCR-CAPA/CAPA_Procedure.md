# Corrective action procedure

## Scenario and purpose
Investigate causes using evidence-based causal analysis; distinguish root cause, contributing factors and symptoms. Actions must address verified causes, with owner, resources, due date and measurable completion criteria.

## Required process and records
Define effectiveness test and observation period before closure. Retain finding ID, containment, cause, action, evidence, verifier and approval.

## Status and limitations
No corrective action is verified complete.

**Portfolio status:** reconstructed simulation for learning purposes. It is not evidence of operational implementation, completed professional audit work, certification, legal compliance or conformity with ISO/IEC 42001.
