# Synthetic evidence and workpaper guide

This repository contains constructed evidence examples and six desk-based workpaper exercises. They are training artefacts, not real operational records.

**Truthful portfolio wording:** “I developed a simulated ISO/IEC 42001 AIMS portfolio for a fictional AI recruitment SaaS, including risk and impact registers, proposed controls, synthetic evidence examples and desk-based audit workpapers.”

Do not claim implementation, real audit execution, control effectiveness, supplier approval, compliance or certification. Evidence hierarchy: (1) real authorised operational records; (2) controlled test artefacts generated by documented tests; (3) synthetic examples; (4) planned evidence descriptions. This repository contains levels 3 and 4 only. The operational evidence tracker must remain Not provided / Not verified.

These workpapers are incomplete training examples: no independent reviewer, real source evidence, interviews or live-system testing. A real audit requires approved scope/programme, criteria, competent independent auditor, documented sampling, objective evidence, reviewed workpapers, report and follow-up.
