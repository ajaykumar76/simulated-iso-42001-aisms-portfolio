# Control mapping limitations

The 14 AIMS-C entries are portfolio control themes, not clause-by-clause ISO/IEC 42001:2023 requirements and not an Annex A applicability statement. AIMS-C labels are internal identifiers, not standard clause numbers.

A defensible requirement-level map requires a legitimate copy of the standard, defined scope, documented applicability decisions and exclusions, linked procedures/records, accountable owners and implementation evidence. Do not infer clause numbers or Annex A control identifiers from these themes. This mapping does not establish conformity.

The EU AI Act is a separate legal framework. A scenario or risk register does not establish a definitive classification or fulfil legal obligations. Use-case-specific legal analysis by competent persons is required.
